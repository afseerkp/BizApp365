I can't sign in

  • Check that you are using the email and password provided by your company administrator.
  • Make sure your device is connected to the internet and the date and time are set automatically.
  • If your password was reset or your account was disabled, ask your company administrator, or contact us with your company name.

My data isn't up to date

  • BizApp365 works on one real-time ledger. Pull down to refresh, or close and reopen the app.
  • If you were offline, reconnect and wait a moment for pending transactions to sync.
  • If figures still differ from the web app, contact support with the document number and time.

Printing and sharing invoices

  • Invoices and receipts can be shared as PDF from the document screen using the share button.
  • For Bluetooth or network printers, make sure the printer is on, paired and selected in the app settings.
  • Contact us if your printer model is not listed — we can help with setup.
